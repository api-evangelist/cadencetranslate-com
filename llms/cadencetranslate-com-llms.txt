# Cadence Translate

> Cadence Translate is a language services provider founded in 2014, delivering interpretation, translation, transcription, and market research to capital markets, expert networks, consultancies, and corporations worldwide.

## Services

- [Expert Networks](https://www.cadencetranslate.com/Expert-Networks): Interpretation and translation support for expert network calls, including IDIs and compliance-sensitive discussions.
- [Transcription](https://www.cadencetranslate.com/Transcription): Call transcription services, including HITL and MTPE workflows.
- [Compliance](https://www.cadencetranslate.com/Compliance): Compliance-grade interpretation and transcription for MNPI-sensitive expert calls and IR meetings.
